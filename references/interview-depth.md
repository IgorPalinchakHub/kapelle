# Question depth

Interview flags were removed. `start` asks no question when repository evidence and the raw task
support a safe plan. For each genuinely blocking decision, it asks a concise standalone question
describing current behavior, the proposal, and its consequence with at most three options.
Do not bundle unrelated decisions or treat a checklist as a question quota. Reuse prior answers;
simple work should not acquire a separate interview or repeated confirmations.

High-risk ambiguity may trigger one critic pass before that question. The agent never runs a fixed
question quota or a default business-analyst/critic/devil's-advocate sequence.
